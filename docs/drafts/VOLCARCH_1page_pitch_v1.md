# VOLCARCH: Unearthing the Invisible Civilisation of Pre-Hindu Java

**A computational framework for recovering what volcanism has hidden**

---

## The Problem

Indonesia's archaeological record begins abruptly around 400 CE with the adoption of Indian writing systems. Before this date, volcanic Java -- home to over 30 active volcanoes and millions of people -- has *zero* confirmed open-air archaeological sites. This is unique globally: no other region with 1M+ years of human occupation has such a complete archaeological blank.

## The Decisive Case

**Non-volcanic West Java has pre-400 CE archaeology. Volcanic East Java has none.**

The Buni Complex (Tangerang, 200 BCE--500 CE) and Batujaya (Karawang, 2nd--5th century CE) prove complex pre-Hindu society existed on Java's non-volcanic coast. Their counterparts in the volcanic interior are *missing*. Same island. Same culture. Same timeframe. Different geology.

## What We've Found (183 computational experiments)

1. **Volcanic sedimentation rates of 2.4--6.2 mm/yr** across 5 independently calibrated sites bury pre-400 CE deposits at 6+ metres -- far beyond standard survey depth.

2. **A settlement suitability model (AUC 0.768)** predicts where buried sites should be, generating 20 GPS-precise targets for geophysical survey.

3. **"Two Javas"**: Sacred architecture (candi) clusters within 15 km of volcanoes; administrative inscriptions cluster 15--30 km away. The textual record of "Indianisation" was a 15-km court phenomenon, not a civilisation-wide transformation.

4. **230 "ghost words"** vanish from inscriptions after the 9th century -- indigenous vocabulary that was *silenced by Sanskrit genre conventions*, not lost. The word for "I" (*aku*) disappears from formal writing after C8.

5. **A new hidden factor: karst.** Volcanic Philippines retains 25 pre-400 CE sites because it has karst caves (0.20 karst fraction). Java's volcanic interior has almost none (0.08). Cave sites bypass the taphonomic cascade.

## Current Status

- **5 papers under review** (EGQSJ, JCAA, Antiquity, Oceanic Linguistics, Archipel)
- **183 experiments**, all open-source (GitHub: volcarch-repo)
- **arXiv preprint live** (2604.00023, Linguistic Fossils)
- **5 testable predictions** with GPS coordinates, depths, and explicit falsification conditions

## The Opportunity

The gap between computational prediction and physical verification is the gap between a hypothesis and a discovery. One GPR campaign at predicted targets could confirm or falsify the entire framework -- and either outcome would be a major contribution.

*"The question is no longer whether pre-Hindu Javanese civilisation existed. The question is how much longer we allow 4 sites per year to be destroyed by construction, undocumented and unrecorded."*
