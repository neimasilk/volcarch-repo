[P3] (1r3) As follows: 4 māṣa of gold, and there shall be no arik, and [the village] will not be impacted by the hopan [of the] royal corvée, varieties of avur…

[P8] (1r8) the Taṅkil, the ataṇḍa (called) saṅ Boṇḍoṅ, native of Vulakan, district of Taṅkilan, ...

[P9] (1r9) district of Lampyar, accompanied by saṅ Maṅuṇḍuh, native of Bākul, district of Lampyar…

[P10] (1r10) district of Vatu Humalaṅ, the scribe (called) saṅ Tañjuṅan, native of Lāḍahu, district of…

[P11] (1r11) native of Paṇḍamuan, district of Vadihati, were/was presented as gifts: … of gold ...
