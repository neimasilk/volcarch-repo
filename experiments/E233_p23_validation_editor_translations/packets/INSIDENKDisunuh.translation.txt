[P1] Hail! Elapsed Śaka year 709, month of Caitra, sixth tithi of the waning fortnight, Friday, Vurukuṅ, Pon. That was when the lord ḍaṅ karayān śrī, the army commander senāpati, restored sīma status to the village of Ḍisunuh for the lord of Humaṇḍiṅ, namely ḍa punta Tīs. Those who mahāla were called: at Ḍisunuh, the lord of Vəka called saṅ ḍa Nui at Ḍisunuh, ḍa punta Maṅuhuri and ḍa punta Tīra at Ḍisunuh, ḍa Tiruan called saṅ Galaha the Ḍisunuh called ḍa punta Rājasiṅhīśvarah saṅ Vayur and saṅ Pravalā The ones who wrote were called: bhagavān Tatā and lord Svasthā.
