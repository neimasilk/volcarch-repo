### Material findings affecting VOLCARCH framing

1. **Facebook-post-triggered Jatim bead claim is fully vindicated** by peer-reviewed primary literature (Jia et al. 2024 Heritage Science Q1).
2. **Methodological caveat identified:** Java attribution for Jatim beads cannot rest on v-Na-Ca chemistry alone. Chinese artisans also produced v-Na-Ca glass locally in Northern Wei period (Jia et al. 2025, vessels). Java attribution depends on full signature: mixed v-Na-Ca + m-Na-Al + eye-bead morphology + specific colorants. Likewise, m-Na-Al in SE Asia is often South Asian-sourced (Wang et al. 2023 Taiwan). **P0 Channel 6 must carefully articulate this.**
3. **Ye-tiao 132 CE Java embassy = mainstream scholarship** (Wolters 1967, Pelliot 1916). The FB post's claim about "Han-era Javanese ships to China 130-ish CE" is not fringe; it is canonical.
4. **Ko-ying (3rd c.) and Kan-t'o-li (5th-6th c.) Sumatra polities** attested in Chinese records. Add to P0 Channel 3 evidence.
