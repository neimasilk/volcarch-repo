## Draft Response Paragraph

> To test the robustness of the cascade model, we performed a Monte Carlo sensitivity analysis (N=100,000) sampling each factor from its full uncertainty range (Table X). The model produces P(visible) within one order of magnitude of the observed archaeological gap in 92% of independent runs (95% CI of implied gap: 233×–5,195×; observed: 3,220×). We further tested the independence assumption using a Gaussian copula with plausible correlations between factors (volcanic burial↔organic decay ρ=0.5; volcanic burial↔survey intensity ρ=−0.4; recognition↔publication ρ=0.3). Under all correlation scenarios, including a worst-case with all correlations active simultaneously, the model-data agreement changes by less than 1% (90.5–93.2% of runs within 10× of observed). The cascade model's conclusion is therefore robust to both parameter uncertainty and the independence assumption.
