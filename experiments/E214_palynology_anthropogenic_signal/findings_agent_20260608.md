## Synthesis (verbatim verdict)
The Java palynological/charcoal record **LEANS AGAINST — at best leaves narrowly OPEN — substantial pre-400 CE human ecological impact.** Every directly-dated Java site placing the question resolves it LATE (Dieng ~600 CE, tied to the Hindu center; Rawa Danau ~AD 1770; review "c. 1500 yr ago"). The single pre-400 CE Java-context signal (Solo marine core ~2950 BP) is hedged and climate-confounded. The contrast is regional not methodological: Sumatra (~7500 BP), Borneo (Niah ~6000 BP) DO show early farming — so Java's lateness is real OR an artifact of severe undersampling (few lowland cores; most highland/marine/climate-dominated). **Verdict: does NOT confirm a hidden pre-400 CE farming population in Java; mostly refutes a LARGE/landscape-scale one; leaves a small window via one ambiguous marine record + acute undersampling.**
