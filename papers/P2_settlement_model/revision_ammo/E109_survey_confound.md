# P2 Revision Ammo: E109 Survey-Burial Confound

**Paper:** JCAA #280
**Date:** 2026-03-17
**New since submission:** YES

---

## The Issue

E109 (forward simulation) revealed that site density INCREASES with burial depth in raw data — counterintuitive for the burial hypothesis. This is because near-volcano zones are simultaneously the most buried AND the most surveyed (shortest road distance: 1,578m vs 9,826m).

## Why This Doesn't Invalidate P2

1. **E069 (ADV-3) already controls for this.** The nested model shows volcanic proximity retains significance (p=0.0015) AFTER controlling road distance and BPCB proximity.

2. **P2's model trains on environmental features, not burial features.** The tautology test (E013-E014) ensures the settlement model predicts suitability, not visibility.

3. **E109's MLE estimates 824 hidden sites** (detection rate 30.2%) — supporting P2's prediction that Zone B/C contain undiscovered sites.

4. **The confound is honest and expected.** Volcanic zones attracted settlement (fertile soil) AND burial (tephra). P2's contribution is separating these effects through tautology-tested modeling.

## Suggested Response

> "We acknowledge that site density and volcanic burial are spatially confounded: volcanic zones are simultaneously the most fertile (attracting settlement) and the most buried (hiding evidence). Forward simulation (E109) confirms this confound produces a positive raw correlation between burial depth and site density. However, our nested model (E069, p=0.0015) isolates a residual volcanic effect after controlling survey intensity proxies, and the tautology test suite (E013-E014) ensures our predictions reflect environmental suitability rather than modern accessibility."
