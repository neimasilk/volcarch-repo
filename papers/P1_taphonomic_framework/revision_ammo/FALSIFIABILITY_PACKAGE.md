## Model Robustness (E115)

Monte Carlo analysis (100,000 runs) shows the cascade model is robust:
- 92% of independent runs fall within 10× of the observed archaeological gap
- All five correlation scenarios (including worst-case) change the median by <1%
- Factor independence is NOT a load-bearing assumption
- Most uncertain parameter: survey coverage (360% range); least uncertain: volcanic burial (60% range)

Monte Carlo sensitivity analysis (100,000 iterations) demonstrates that this prediction is robust to parameter uncertainty — 92% of runs produce visibility estimates within one order of magnitude of the observed gap, and the result is invariant to correlated factor assumptions. We note that all known pre-400 CE archaeological sites in Java are in taphonomically protected contexts (caves, river terraces, or coastal locations); the volcanic interior record begins only at depths accessible to historical survey methods. The framework's practical contribution is spatial targeting: volcanic-informed survey is 3.5× more efficient than random deployment, with cost savings of approximately US$83,000 to achieve statistical significance (≥5 finds).
