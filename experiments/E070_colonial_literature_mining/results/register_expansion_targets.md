## REMARKABLE FINDINGS

1. **Singasari 4 lost temples:** Drawn by Bik in 1822, entirely underground by 1920s = burial rate of ~2-3m per century. This is the STRONGEST evidence for rapid volcanic sedimentation.

2. **Garahan ash layer:** DIRECT volcanic stratigraphy — 18cm Raoen ash layer identified as a marker horizon above a megalithic grave. This is exactly the kind of tephra-sealed archaeological deposit we need.

3. **Tjandi Pendem lahar layers:** "Zich tusschen de vele laharlagen drie 'parang' lagen bevonden van groote hardheid" — MULTIPLE lahar layers identified during excavation, forming hard "parang" strata. Quantitative stratigraphy.

4. **Tjandi Sawentar subsidiaries ON lahar sand:** Buildings FOUNDED on lahar deposits, while the main temple sits on clay below = lahar event BETWEEN construction phases. Datable volcanic event.

5. **Majapahit as reclamation of lahar-devastated land (OV 1929):** Maclaine Pont's hypothesis that Majapahit was founded on land previously devastated by lahars = volcanic destruction as a RECURRING cycle.
