Dear Prof. Verberne,

As promised, please find attached the research proposal (7 pages).

The proposal outlines a cumulative PhD (3 new papers) on NLP for colonial Dutch archives, structured around four research questions: NER for historical Dutch (RQ1), temporal information extraction (RQ2), place-name disambiguation (RQ3), and physical validation of textual extractions against the VOLCARCH sedimentation model (RQ4). The thesis is designed as a direct extension of LIACS's "Digging in Documents" and EXALT research lines — from modern excavation reports to the primary colonial sources themselves.

Section 7 summarises pilot results that demonstrate feasibility: a Delpher extraction pipeline (1,768 records), an OV text mining experiment (22,162 structured mentions from 16 volumes), and a cross-century depth validation (33 colonial records consistent with the burial model, Wilcoxon p = 0.131).

I have kept the proposal self-contained so it can be read independently of the VOLCARCH brief I sent earlier. Happy to clarify or adjust scope based on your reading.
