# Revision Ammo: Karst as Hidden Factor (E178)

**Use when:** Reviewer asks "Why is Java uniquely dark?" or "Philippines is also volcanic but has pre-400 CE sites."

## Key Point

E178 reveals that karst availability is a hidden factor. Philippine volcanic zones have 25 pre-400 CE sites because they have extensive karst (caves that bypass all 5 cascade factors). Java's volcanic interior has very little karst (0.08 vs 0.20).

## Suggested Addition to Limitations

> "A comparative analysis of volcanic regions across Southeast Asia suggests that karst availability is a confounding factor: volcanic zones with extensive karst (e.g., the Philippines) retain pre-400 CE cave sites even where open-air sites are absent. Java's volcanic interior has relatively little karst (karst fraction ~0.08 vs ~0.20 in Philippine volcanic zones), which may contribute to its uniquely sparse pre-Hindu record beyond volcanism alone. The cascade model should be formally augmented with a karst bypass term."
