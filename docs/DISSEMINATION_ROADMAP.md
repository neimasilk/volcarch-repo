## WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

VOLCARCH has 120 experiments, 5+ papers ready/submitted, and strong evidence. The bottleneck is no longer scientific — it's social. The right people need to know, care, and act. Traditional journal publications alone reach ~200 readers each. We need 100x that to trigger fieldwork.

**Goal:** Create enough visibility and compelling materials that:
1. A fieldwork partner emerges (archaeologist with permits)
2. A funder emerges (grant, corporate, or crowdfunding)
3. A LiDAR survey happens at VOLCARCH's predicted targets
4. Indonesian rescue archaeology gets political attention

## UNCONVENTIONAL IDEAS (Revisit Periodically)

### Prediction Registry
- Publicly register 10 location predictions with timestamps on GitHub/Zenodo
- When someone eventually digs there and finds something → VOLCARCH is validated
- Builds credibility like physics predictions (Higgs boson was predicted decades before found)

### Construction Company Data Partnership
- Infrastructure projects (toll roads, dams, MRT) drill deep boreholes in Java
- They already have soil profiles that might contain "anomalous" layers
- MoU: "May we examine your soil profile data from zone [X]?"
- Zero cost. Potentially transformative.

### Low-Cost Deep Coring Campaign
- Commercial geotechnical borehole: ~$200-500 per 10m core
- 20 cores x $300 = $6,000
- Not archaeology proper, but enough to confirm cultural layers at predicted depths
- Could be self-funded or crowdfunded

### "Bounty" for Accidental Finds
- Informal network with contractors in priority zones
- "If you find something while digging >3m, call us. We'll help you report properly."
- Needs BPCB partnership for legitimacy
