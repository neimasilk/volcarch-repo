# E235 adversarial audit (2026-10-20)

Auditor: Claude (subagent), read-only on the repo. All scripts are in this folder (`a1_prepare.py` …
`a10_sanity.py`). The cached cell table is `cells.parquet`. Every check marked **post hoc** below was not

## 0. Bottom line

- **The code implements DESIGN.md faithfully.** No deviation could change a verdict, and no bug was found.
  My independent re-derivation reproduces every stored point estimate to 4–5 decimals, and the bootstrap CIs to
  about ±0.002.
- **The primary M2 verdict (INCONCLUSIVE) sits on a knife edge, and reasonable choices push it both ways.**
  - XGB random and TGB are 0.0009 and 0.0011 below δ, with CIs that exclude 0.
  - Using E219's own 13-centre set, or N ≥ 50 km, makes all three designs SPECIFIC.
  - Matching on river distance (a model feature that was left unmatched) or using finer bins makes all three
    NOT SPECIFIC, with Δ̄ falling to +0.012–0.028.
- **Three README sentences overreach** (§4): "vanishes when the volcanic zone is drawn tighter", "such terrain
  does not exist there", and "the designs' disagreement does not depend on volcanic setting". The figure
  description also overreaches: "about 0.1" and "about 25 %" hold only above about 1,600 m.
- **Strongest threats** (§5):
  1. The M3 thresholds cannot be reached. 5 pp is larger than the V-arm fringe rate itself (1.7 %), so NOT
     SPECIFIC was close to guaranteed.
  2. Spatial confounding. Above 400 m, 86 % of the matched non-volcanic weight is the western Southern Mountains,
     which are, to my knowledge, Tertiary volcanic rock. Dropping the north-coast N cells halves Δ̄.
  3. M2's fragility (above).

## 2. Bug checks

- **Elevation ≤ 0 and NaN.** `build_frame` drops NaN in any feature or road_dist, and drops elevation ≤ 0. The minimum
  elevation is 0.0001 m and no NaNs remain, so there is no band −1 and no NaN band.
- **Off-by-one.** Band b = [200b, 200b+200). E\* = 1,000 and "above" means elev ≥ 1,000, which is consistent.
- **CRS.**
  - Cells are in EPSG:32749 (the DEM CRS was checked), and the centres are projected with pyproj into the same CRS.
  - Euclidean UTM distance differs from geodesic haversine by at most 0.65 km (0.58 %).
  - 1,713 cells change arm. Δ̄ for random moves 0.0491 → 0.0489, away from δ.
- **Block ids.** `int(x/49,950)·10⁴ + int(y/49,950)`. UTM coordinates are positive, so truncation equals floor.
  The matched table has 20 V blocks and 35 N blocks.
- **Emptied strata in the bootstrap.** w = 0 removes a stratum. If an arm loses a whole band in a replicate,
  the band is NaN and Δ̄ uses the remaining bands. For band 800–1,000 this happens in about 0.1 % of replicates,
  which is negligible.
- **Fringe.** I recomputed `n_designs_top10` from the three XGB ensembles (top 10 % of all 588,535 cells) and it
  agrees with the stored values in 100 % of cells. Fringe = n == 1, as in E221.
- **M3a scaling.** It is correct: percent ×100, so δ = 5 pp.

**No bug found.**

## 4. README statements: supported or not

- **"the comparison … cannot be made: such terrain does not exist there" — overreaches.**
  - The terrain exists: 15 km² of N at 1,000–1,200 m, in 4 blocks. It is simply below the 25 km² rule.
  - E\* depends on that rule: it is 1,200 m with a minimum ≤ 14.9 km², 1,000 m for 15–185 km², and 800 m from
    ≥ 190 km² (a6).
  - Those cells are not "non-volcanic" in a geological sense. They sit in the Pacitan–Ponorogo Southern Mountains,
    with about 1 km² on Ringgit–Beser.
  - Suggested wording: "too little to compare (15 km² at 1,000–1,200 m, none above 1,200 m)".
- **"vanishes when the volcanic zone is drawn tighter" (and "disappears" in the Conclusion) — wrong attribution.**
  The 10/30 run changes three things at once: a tighter V, a looser N, and an extra band. Decomposition for XGB
  (random / TGB / hybrid):

  | Run | Δ̄ |
  |---|---|
  | Tighter V only (≤ 10, N ≥ 40) | +0.029 / +0.032 / +0.035 |
  | V ≤ 15, N ≥ 40 | +0.041 / +0.042 / +0.050 |
  | Looser N only (V ≤ 20, N ≥ 30) | +0.029 / +0.029 / +0.039 |
  | Both changes, bands 0–1,000 only | +0.005 / +0.008 / +0.010 |
  | Stored 10/30 run (the 1,000–1,200 m band adds band Δ −0.18) | −0.002 |
  | **Stricter N (≥ 50)** | **+0.056 / +0.056 / +0.067, all SPECIFIC** |

  Δ̄ tracks how far away the N arm is. Tightening V alone roughly halves it; it does not remove it. The CI of the
  10/30 run (−0.047 to +0.046) also still contains the primary estimate.
- **"positive in every band" — true** for XGB, for all three designs, as point estimates. Two of the five band CIs
  for random (600–800 and 800–1,000 m) include 0. MaxEnt random and TGB are negative at 800–1,000 m.
- **"NOT SPECIFIC for all three algorithms and all three definitions" (M3) — literally true by the rule, but see
  §5.1.**
  - For XGB and RF the CIs exclude 0 (positive); for MaxEnt M3b the CI excludes 0 (negative).
  - "Below 1,000 m, the designs' disagreement does not depend on volcanic setting" overreaches. The matched fringe
    rate is V 1.73 % against N 1.16 %, a ratio of 1.48.
- **Figure, above 1,000 m (XGB V arm, unmatched band means) — partly overstated.**

  | Band | random | hybrid | fringe |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1,000–1,200 m | 0.50 | 0.60 | 10 % |

  "Hybrid near 0.5" is fine. "Random/TGB fall to about 0.1" and "fringe about 25 %" hold only above about
  1,600 m. The designs do not part company until about 1,200–1,400 m.
- **"East Java" — imprecise.**
  - The frame spans 110.90–114.60°E. It includes about 157 km² of Lawu's Central Java flank above 1,000 m, plus the
    Rembang/Blora and Wonogiri strips.
  - It also includes 388 km² of west Bali, all N or excluded. Its effect on Δ̄ is negligible (+0.0003).
  - The text should say "study area".
- **M4 and M5 numbers, the area figures, "0.0 %" (actually 0.02 %), the matched-cell count and the G1 claim** are
  all supported.

## 5. Threats

1. **The M3 thresholds cannot be reached — serious for the M3 sentence.**
   - Below E\*, fringe cells make up 1.7 % (V) and 1.2 % (N) of matched cells.
   - Any disagreement at all (n ∈ {1, 2}): V 3.57 % against N 2.44 %, Δ +1.13 pp.
   - A 5 pp Δ would need a V rate ≥ 5 %, so NOT SPECIFIC was close to pre-determined.
   - M3b has the same problem: δ = 0.05 is larger than the N-arm mean \|h − r\| itself (0.038).
   - The M3 rule therefore cannot be called a test, even though it was pre-registered.
   - Report instead: "rare in both settings; about 1.5× higher near volcanoes; absolute difference < 1 pp".
2. **Spatial confounding — serious (post hoc, a4).**
   - Above 400 m, 86 % of the matched N weight lies in the western Southern Mountains box (Pacitan–south Malang).
     In bands 600–1,000 m the top 3 N blocks hold 92–94 % of the weight, so there are effectively about 3 clusters.
   - Leave one region out (XGB random, primary 0.049):
     - drop N in the north (Kendeng–Rembang–Tuban box) → **+0.027**;
     - drop N in the Southern Mountains → **+0.058**;
     - drop N in Madura → +0.050;
     - drop N on the eastern south coast → +0.049.
   - The N-block jackknife is mild (0.0425–0.0515), but region-level dependence is strong.
   - Δ̄ is therefore largely "volcanic flanks vs northern lowland hills" and is not a setting effect that can be
     generalised.
3. **Fragility of M2 and residual imbalance — serious (post hoc, a3/a6/a7).**
   - Within the strata, V cells are still 22 m higher.
   - Finer CEM (XGB random / TGB / hybrid, with block-bootstrap CI):

     | Matching | Δ̄ | CI (random) | Verdict |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | 200 m bands, quintiles (E219 used quintiles) | +0.036 / +0.036 / +0.046 | — | INC |
     | + river-distance terciles | +0.028 / +0.029 / +0.033 | [+0.020, +0.037] | NOT SPECIFIC (all 3) |
     | 100 m bands, quintiles + river quintiles | +0.012 / +0.012 / +0.014 | — | NOT SPECIFIC |

   - The other way: E219's 13 in-bounds centres give +0.0505 / +0.0503 / +0.0620, SPECIFIC ×3.
   - The README's "+0.05 … terrain-matched" mainly measures coarse tercile bins and unmatched river distance.
     That is consistent with "terrain, not setting", but a reviewer can move the label in either direction.
4. **Lithology — moderate.**
   - The distance rule calls the Southern Mountains "non-volcanic". To my knowledge, from memory, these are an
     Oligo-Miocene volcanic arc.
   - N cells also reach 1,132 m on Ringgit–Beser (Situbondo, 42 km from Argapura) and 665 m near Pandan (40 km from
     Wilis). I believe both are Pleistocene centres, though neither is in the 30-centre list.
   - **These geology identifications are mine and have not been checked against a geological map. Verify them.**
   - The text should say "far from Quaternary centres", never "non-volcanic".
5. **25 km² and ≥ 2 blocks — weak rules, low practical risk.**
   - E\* is stable from 15 to 185 km².
   - The upland bands rest on about 3 effective N clusters, so the per-band CIs there are unreliable.
6. **δ chosen knowing +0.055 — declared, low risk on its own.** It matters because of item 3: the estimates land
   within 0.001 of δ.
