## Version History

## Data Sources
All entries are extracted from *Oudheidkundig Verslag* (OV) reports, the annual publication of the Netherlands East Indies Archaeological Service (Oudheidkundige Dienst in Nederlandsch-Indie). The OV reports were published by the Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen and are available on Internet Archive.

- Median: 1.75 m (measured subset only, n=27: median 2.00 m)

## Limitations
1. OCR quality varies; some site names may have transcription errors
2. Coordinates approximate (±5-20 km depending on location specificity)
3. Not all depth measurements represent natural burial (some are architectural features)
4. Coverage biased toward Hindu-Buddhist brick/stone structures
5. Volumes 1913, 1915 not available; post-1929 not yet processed
