## Query 5: `ancient DNA Java Indonesia recovered Neolithic Holocene`

**Target:** Counter to "no aDNA from volcanic Java" claim

**Result:** **MATERIAL QUALIFIER — Channel 4 needs reframing, not deletion.**

Key finding: **Leang Panninge South Sulawesi, aDNA recovered, 7.3-7.2 thousand years ago** (Nature 2021; Science 2018; multiple papers).

**This is aDNA from INDONESIA** — limestone cave, Sulawesi. The claim "no aDNA from Indonesia" would be false. The claim "no aDNA from volcanic Java open-air contexts" remains true.

**Critical implication for P0 Channel 4:**

The aDNA absence is **not independent evidence of taphonomic erasure in Java**. It's PART OF the karst-vs-open-air preservation differential already documented (Philippines comparison, karst-hidden-factor E178). Leang Panninge's recovery directly demonstrates that cave sites in Indonesian limestone preserve aDNA while open-air volcanic zones don't.

**Must reframe P0 §3.4:**

*Before:* "aDNA has never been recovered from volcanic Java despite deep population history evidenced in modern genomes" → implies unique evidence for taphonomic burial

*After:* "aDNA from Indonesia has been recovered exclusively from limestone cave contexts (Leang Panninge, Sulawesi, 7.3 kya). No aDNA has been recovered from any volcanic-Java open-air context. This is predicted by the karst-vs-open-air preservation differential (E178) and consistent with but does not independently confirm taphonomic erasure of pre-Hindu settlement material."

**This is the only material finding from 5 counter-queries.** It does not break VOLCARCH — it refines it. Channel 4 weakens from "independent evidence" to "consistent with karst differential."
