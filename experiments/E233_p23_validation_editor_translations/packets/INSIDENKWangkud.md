# INSIDENKWangkud — Demarcation of Asam Panjang (874 CE)

**Responsibility (TEI header):** EpiDoc Encoding Arlo Griffiths; intellectual authorship of edition Arlo Griffiths
**Translation language:** eng (not declared)

## Translation (verbatim from the DHARMA edition; paragraph number and line reference in [ ])

[P1 | n=1-5] Hail! Elapsed Śaka-year 786, month of Āṣāḍha, fifth of the waning fortnight, Paniruan, Wage, Wednesday, lunar mansion Uttarāṣāḍha, conjunction Saubhāgya.

[P2 | n=5-9] At that time the venerable one pitāmaha of Vaṅkud planted the boundary markers for Asam Panjang, district of Pikatan. The village owning official at that time was Manuku, native of the village .
