## Original Critique (C2)

> "P1, P17, P18 overlap BANYAK. 'Volcanic burial hides civilization' (P1), 'Two Javas divided by geology' (P17), 'Invisible civilization' (P18) — ini basically tiga paper yang mengatakan hal yang sama dari angle berbeda."

## Revised Assessment After Close Reading

The three papers form a **trilogy with distinct contributions**, not duplicates:

| Paper | Core Question | Method | Audience |
|-------|-------------|--------|----------|
| P1 | HOW DEEP is the burial? | Geoarchaeological calibration (4 sites, sedimentation rates) | Quaternary scientists, geoarchaeologists |
| P17 | WHERE does the bias act differently? | Spatial statistics (candi vs inscription distribution) | Computational archaeologists |
| P18 | WHAT was hidden? | ML substrate detection + vocabulary classification | Cultural anthropologists, linguists |

### Why this is NOT salami-slicing:

1. **Different methods:** P1 = physical measurement, P17 = spatial analysis, P18 = NLP/ML
2. **Different datasets:** P1 = 4 calibration sites + 666 site database, P17 = 142 candi + 176 inscriptions, P18 = 438 substrate vocabulary + 25,244 village names
3. **Different audiences:** A geoarchaeologist reading EGQSJ does not read Archeologia e Calcolatori or World Archaeology
4. **Different journals:** P1→EGQSJ (Copernicus), P17→Archeologia e Calcolatori (CNR), P18→TBD (HOLD)

### Remaining overlap risk:

- The cascade model (E110) appears in both P1 and P18. P1 presents it as supporting evidence; P18 uses it as a core analytical tool. Solution: P18 should cite P1 for the cascade model rather than re-deriving it.
- West Java comparison appears in both P1 and P18. Same solution: P18 cites P1.
- Self-citation chain will be visible to reviewers. Mitigate by:
  - Making each paper self-contained (can be read without the others)
  - Being explicit about the relationship: "This is the third paper in a series investigating..."
  - Ensuring each paper has independent contributions that survive without the others

### Recommendation REVISED:

Do NOT merge P17 and P18. They are complementary, not redundant.
DO space submissions 4-6 weeks apart.
DO make P18 cite P1 for cascade model and West Java comparison.
