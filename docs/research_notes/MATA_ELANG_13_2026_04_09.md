## 1. SEVEN STRUCTURAL RISKS

### RISK 1: THE CASCADE IS UNFALSIFIABLE (SEVERITY: CRITICAL)

**The problem:** E110's 5-factor cascade (F1=0.58, F2=0.20, F3=0.025, F4=0.40, F5=0.50) has 5 free parameters and 1 data point (the observed 0.031% visibility). This is radically underdetermined. E115's Monte Carlo shows **92% of random parameter combinations also bracket the gap** — the model accepts almost anything.

**The deeper problem:** Every possible observation can be absorbed:
- Sites found? → "F3 was underestimated"
- No sites found? → "Model confirmed"
- Sites well-preserved? → "F2 was overestimated"
- Sites deeply buried? → "F1 was overestimated"

This is not science. It's a **narrative framework with numbers attached**.

**E155's "validation" is circular.** Both the predicted factors AND the "observed" values for 5 regions were estimated by the same analyst (Claude). Spearman rho=1.0 on N=5 is impressive-looking but the whole exercise is Claude comparing its own estimates to its own estimates.

**Recommendation:** 
1. **STOP claiming the cascade "matches data" or is "validated."** Instead, frame it as: "A plausible mechanistic decomposition consistent with observations." This is honest and still publishable.
2. **The cascade's ONLY predictive test:** Estimate factors for a NEW region BEFORE fieldwork, then compare. Until then, it's post-hoc rationalization.
3. **A 2-factor model (survey deficit × combined taphonomy) explains the data equally well.** Parsimony demands acknowledging this. The 5-factor decomposition is pedagogically useful but scientifically underdetermined.

### RISK 5: THE ECHO CHAMBER HAS NOT BEEN BROKEN (SEVERITY: CRITICAL)

ME#12 identified this. Nothing has changed:

```
Claude proposes hypothesis
    → Claude designs experiment
        → Claude writes code
            → Claude interprets results
                → Claude declares SUCCESS (79% rate)
                    → Claude writes paper citing the experiment

```

At no point does an independent mind challenge the methodology. The Mata Elang reviews are Claude auditing Claude. The "external reviews" for P11 were solicited by the same workflow and applied by the same agent.

**The 79% SUCCESS rate is suspiciously high for exploratory research.** Compare to published meta-analyses: psychology replication rate ~36%, economics ~61%, even physics ~70%. A 79% rate either means VOLCARCH's hypotheses are trivially true, or there's systematic confirmation bias.

**What would break the echo chamber:**
1. **Get P17 through double-blind review at ArchCalc.** This is THE most important gate.
2. **Make GitHub repo truly public** and actively invite scrutiny (post on Reddit r/archaeology, Mastodon, relevant mailing lists).
3. **Seek out an adversarial reviewer** — a Indonesian archaeologist or volcanologist who would WANT to prove VOLCARCH wrong. Pay them $50-100 for a 2-page review.

- **Pak Amien** has deep contextual knowledge but may not have the statistical background to challenge Claude's methodology independently.

- Structural equation models (appropriate for cascade dependencies)
- Causal inference frameworks (appropriate for the "volcanism causes invisibility" claim)

**Recommendation:** For the strongest papers (P17, P1), hire a statistics reviewer (~$50 on Fiverr/academic freelance). One afternoon of expert review would catch methodological blind spots that 175 experiments of self-review cannot.

## 3. FIVE EXECUTABLE EXPERIMENTS (This Session)

### E176: Cascade Falsifiability Test — Minimal Model Comparison

**Hypothesis:** A 2-factor model (survey deficit × combined taphonomy) explains the data as well as the 5-factor cascade.

**Method:**
1. Compute P(visible) for 2-factor, 3-factor, 4-factor, and 5-factor models
2. Compare each to observed 0.031%
3. Compute Akaike Information Criterion analog: does adding factors improve fit enough to justify complexity?
4. For each reduced model, check if it still brackets observations

**Prediction:** The 2-factor model will ALSO bracket the gap, exposing the 5-factor model as over-parameterized.

**Value:** Forces honest reframing. If 2 factors suffice, stop pretending 5 gives precision.

### E177: GEBCO Sunda Shelf Paleo-Drainage Reconstruction

**Hypothesis:** Paleo-river systems on the drowned Sunda Shelf can be computationally reconstructed from bathymetry data, identifying the most likely pre-Holocene settlement corridors.

**Method:**
1. Download GEBCO 2023 bathymetry tiles for Sunda Shelf
2. Apply flow accumulation algorithms to identify paleo-drainages at -120m (LGM sea level)
3. Map settlement corridor probability based on: river proximity, slope, distance to coast at -120m
4. Identify Java entry points where Sunda refugees would arrive

**Value:** First computational L2 model. Genuinely novel. Could be its own paper (P23 or expand P18).

### E178: Philippines Archaeological Density Regression

**Hypothesis:** Java's pre-400 CE archaeological darkness is UNIQUELY volcanic, not a pan-Austronesian or tropical phenomenon.

**Method:**
1. Compile pre-400 CE site counts for: Java, Bali, Philippines (volcanic vs non-volcanic islands), Sulawesi, Peninsular Malaysia
2. Multiple regression: site_count ~ volcanic_density + karst_fraction + survey_intensity + latitude
3. Test whether volcanic_density retains significance after controlling for other factors

**Value:** Hostile reviewer's first question: "Is this volcanic or just tropical?" This answers it quantitatively.

### E179: Cascade Factor Independence Test

**Hypothesis:** F1 (volcanic burial) and F2 (organic decay) are NOT independent — burial creates anaerobic conditions that SLOW decay. The cascade overestimates combined loss.

**Method:**
1. Literature review: organic preservation in volcanic vs non-volcanic tropical contexts
2. Compute conditional probability: P(decayed | buried) vs P(decayed | not_buried)
3. Recompute cascade with coupled F1×F2 factor
4. Compare to independent-factor assumption

**Value:** Directly addresses the weakest mechanistic assumption. Could either strengthen (if coupling is weak) or fundamentally revise (if coupling is strong) the model.

### E180: Delpher NLP Pilot — Colonial Archaeological NER

**Hypothesis:** Dutch colonial records contain systematically extractable archaeological data that is independent from DHARMA inscriptions.

**Method:**
1. Download 100 Delpher pages mentioning "oudheidkundig" OR "archeologisch" in Java context
2. Build simple NER rules (spaCy + regex) for: site names, depths, materials, dates
3. Validate against E070 manual dataset (52 entries, expect >90% recall)
4. Extract NEW entries not in E070

**Value:** Breaks DHARMA monoculture. NLP = user's core expertise. Extends E091. Directly feeds P1 revision.
