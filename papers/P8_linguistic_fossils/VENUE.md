small-capital run-in headings; acknowledgments and funding in footnote 1; about 34 numbered tables with captions in
capitals; correspondence tables give the proto-form with an asterisk first, then the reflexes, then the gloss in single
quotes; ISO 639-3 codes in square brackets at first mention of a language; the conclusion rests the case on a bundle
of evidence and says where a single item is weak.
