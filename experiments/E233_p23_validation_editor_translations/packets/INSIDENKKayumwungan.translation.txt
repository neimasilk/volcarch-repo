[P1 | n=12] That temple shines as wide as a slice of the Moon instantly fallen to the earth as though for fear of Rāhu. Or rather, the white one śubhra masc., i.e., the Moon rises above this lovely temple that shares features with Him, inhabited by Buddhas both ... and young.

[P2 | n=14] Once it is concentrated upon by me, may I just quickly reach that stage, extremely difficult to attain for his sons i.e, bodhisattvas, that is ultimate, beyond the senses.
