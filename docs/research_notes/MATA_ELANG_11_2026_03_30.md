## 2. KRITIK KERAS (Harsh Structural Critique)

### FATAL RISKS

**F1: E137 breaks E110 cascade model.**
E137 (Accidental Discovery Rate) reveals that recognition factor F4 should be ~0.0007, not 0.40 — a 570× discrepancy. If applied to E110's cascade:
- E110 with F4=0.40: predicts 0.058% visibility → fits observed 0.031% (1.9×)
- E110 with F4=0.0007: predicts 0.0001% visibility → 300× below observed

This is an internal contradiction. Either:
(a) E137's model is flawed (most likely — its own 600× overprediction reveals modeling error)
(b) F4 means different things in accidental vs. systematic contexts
(c) The cascade factors compensate non-independently

**Resolution required:** Explicitly document that F4 in the cascade represents systematic archaeological practice, not accidental discovery. E137's F4 applies to sand miners finding pottery, not to trained archaeologists interpreting GPR results. These are categorically different processes.

**F2: Bayes Factor of 72 billion is a credibility time bomb.**
E136's composite BF of 72,000,000,000:1 will destroy credibility in peer review. Individual BFs are ESTIMATED by Claude, not computed from likelihood ratios. A reviewer will ask "where do these come from?" and the answer undermines the entire exercise.

**Resolution required:** Reframe E136 as "illustrative Bayesian framework showing evidence converges" — NOT as "proof the thesis is 100% correct." Never cite BF=72B in a paper. The qualitative conclusion (evidence strongly converges) is valid; the number is not.

**F3: 28 experiments in one day = quality over quantity concern.**
Not all 28 entries are hypothesis tests:

| Type | Count | Examples |
|------|-------|---------|
| Genuine hypothesis tests | 12 | E120-E123, E128, E130, E134, E137, E145-E147 |
| Robustness/validation | 3 | E121, E122, E135 |
| Compilations/databases | 4 | E126, E127, E140, E141-E143 |
| Synthesis/figures | 3 | E133, E144, E119 (already existed) |
| Planning/methodology | 2 | E138, E139 |
| Empty/superseded | 2 | E124, E125 |
| NLP pipeline | 2 | E142, E143 |

The 79% SUCCESS rate is inflated because compilations and syntheses cannot "fail." Genuine hypothesis tests: 12/28 (43%). Of those, 10 succeed, 1 partial (E137), 1 info-neg (E145). More honest success rate for hypothesis tests: 83%.

### STRUCTURAL RISKS

**S1: E145 contradicts L6 interpretation.**
E145 finds eruption frequency POSITIVELY correlates with inscriptions (ρ=+0.908, p=0.0001). More eruptions per century = more inscriptions, not fewer. This directly contradicts L6 ("Historiographic Periodicity = eruptions cause dark centuries").

Meanwhile, E078 found 6.3× deficit near eruptions (p=0.035). These are not contradictory IF properly framed:
- E078 = SPATIAL effect (proximity to eruption kills inscriptions)
- E145 = TEMPORAL effect (centuries with powerful kingdoms document both inscriptions AND eruptions)

**Resolution required:** L6 must be reframed. The periodicity in the record is driven by political cycles (Mataram rise/fall, Kahuripan transition, Singosari era), not by eruption frequency. Eruptions may accelerate political collapse, but the correlation is confounded by state power.

**S2: Dataset monoculture persists.**
Of 28 new experiments:
- 6 reuse DHARMA inscriptions (E130, E134, E140, E146, E147 + E142 partial)
- 4 reuse E001 site database (E120, E122, E129, E132)
- 3 reuse E083 burial data (E128, E132, E135)
- Only 4 introduce genuinely new data: E141-E143 (Delpher), E128 (OV colonial)

**The DHARMA dependency is now at ~25 experiments out of 148 (~17%).** The Delpher pipeline is promising but yielded thin results (48 finds, 1 with depth data).

**S3: Delpher pipeline promising but thin.**
Headlines (529 records, 48 finds) mask reality:
- Only 1/48 finds has depth information (the critical variable for VOLCARCH)
- 13/33 georeferenced finds (39%) overlap with E080 targets
- Phase 2 (full-text NLP) hasn't extracted depth data at scale
- P21 (ColonialMine) cannot be written from current data

**S4: Zero external validation after 148 experiments.**
All 148 experiments are Claude-generated computational analyses. 3 papers rejected, 3 under review, 0 accepted. No independent researcher has replicated any finding. The Bayesian posterior of "~100%" (E136) is meaningless without external validation.

**S5: Cascade model remains unfalsifiable in practice.**
E116 defined testable predictions (20 GPR → [0,6] finds, $40-100K). E138/E139 designed fieldwork strategies. But no funding, no fieldwork team, no institutional access. The model fits beautifully but cannot be TESTED without resources the project doesn't have.

### BLIND SPOTS

**B1: L2 (Coastal Submersion) — 1 experiment in 148.**
Only E052 addresses L2. Zero experiments on: sea-level archaeological sites, coastal erosion rates, marine survey gaps, coral reef dating. This is 1/6 of the manifesto with <1% of experiments.

**B2: No physical science integration.**
Zero experiments on: phytoliths (despite I-125 TESTABLE), isotopes, pottery composition, metallurgy, soil chemistry. All work is computational/textual. The project needs a single phytolith analysis or GPR survey more than 10 more NLP experiments.

**B3: No ethnographic analogy.**
Zero experiments on modern volcanic communities — how Tenggerese, Merapi villagers, or Flores communities interact with volcanic landscapes and what material culture they produce/preserve. This could calibrate F2 (organic decay) and F4 (recognition) with real data.

**B4: Post-929 CE period under-examined.**
The "Two Javas" model (P17) focuses on pre/post 929 CE shift. But the mechanism of the shift (Merapi 928 eruption? political collapse? trade route change?) has only E099 (INCONCLUSIVE). The post-929 eastern Java period could be a natural experiment for volcanic burial rates.
