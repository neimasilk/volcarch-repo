## 4. Channel Independence Analysis

P0's argument rests on *independent* channels converging, not on any single channel. The SLR allows a sharper independence statement:

| Channel | Data origin | Method origin | Researcher community | Time period of data collection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Sedimentation | Javanese candi burial depths + colonial photo archives | Tephrochronology, geoarchaeology | Indonesian + Dutch archaeologists, field geologists | 1803-present |
| 2 Philippines | Philippines archaeology literature | Comparative archaeology | Philippines-focused archaeologists (Bellwood, Dizon, Mijares) | 1960s-present |
| 3 Linguistic | ABVD cognate database, DHARMA inscriptions, OJW, kakawin literature | Historical linguistics, NLP | Austronesian linguists (Blust, Adelaar), Indologists | 19th c.-present |
| 4 Paleogenomic | aDNA, WGS | Paleogenomics | Geneticists (Carlhoff, Lipson, Maulana, Larena) | 2010s-present |
| 5 Colonial archive | OV 1925-1949, Delpher 1854-1942 | NLP on Dutch colonial text | Dutch colonial archaeology (historical); VOLCARCH (contemporary) | 1854-2026 |
| **6 Archaeometric** | **Extra-Javanese terminal sites (China, Korea, Egypt, Micronesia) + Bali bronze** | **LA-ICP-MS, lead isotopes, SEM-EDS** | **Chinese archaeometrists, Korean archaeologists, Roman Red Sea excavators, bronze specialists** | **1960s-2024** |

Channel 6 is the most independent of all six — it uses data collected far outside Indonesia, by researchers studying China / Korea / Egypt, for reasons unrelated to Javanese archaeology. It is also the most methodologically heterogeneous (glass chemistry + bronze lead isotopes). Its status as a post-hoc discovery for VOLCARCH (Pak Amien's Facebook-post trigger → SLR verification) makes it a *natural experiment* in channel independence.
