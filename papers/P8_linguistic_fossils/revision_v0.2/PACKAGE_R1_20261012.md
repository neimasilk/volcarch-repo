## 4. The journal's rules and template

- **Reference list.** Until v0.3 it was set by pandoc with the Unified Style Sheet for Linguistics. The template asks for the
  journal's own form of Chicago author-date. `ol_refs.py` now sets it from the two `.bib` files: "Journal vol:pages" without
  issue number, "In *Title*, ed. by …, pages. Series. City: Publisher.", theses, abbreviated inclusive page numbers, serial
  comma, three em-dashes for a repeated author, italics for journal, book and collection titles, no DOI.
- **House style in the text** (`make_v04_from_v03.py`, stage 1): serial comma (36 places), numerals for numbers of two digits
  (22), lower-case "section/table" inside a sentence (4), closed-up prefixes (recoded, rerunning, preglottalized, rederivation,
  protoform, protolanguage), commas inside closing double quotes, the en-dash as minus sign.
- **Layout** (`build_docx.py`): headings run on into the text (Word's style separator, as the template does it); tables without
  grid, alternate rows shaded, a rule under the last row, columns fitted to contents, notes in "OL footnote" with † ‡ #;
  position markers `<figure n here>`; Figure 1's marker at its first mention so that the figures stand in numerical order;
  signed numbers in table cells do not wrap.
- **Acknowledgments are left out of the anonymized file** (instructions for contributors); the named file keeps them; in the
  final version they belong in the first note.
- Left as they are, deliberately: straight quotation marks (the figures have them too; a typesetter's job); "ABVD" in the
  abstract (defined there, and in the title); forward mentions of Tables 5 and 3 before Table 1.
