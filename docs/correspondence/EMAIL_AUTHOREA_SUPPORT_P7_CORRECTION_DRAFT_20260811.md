Dear Authorea Support team,

I am the corresponding author of the preprint "Spatial segregation of deep-time archaeological sites from volcanic plains in East Java: evidence for taphonomic burial" (DOI 10.22541/au.177368991.14332505/v1, posted 16 March 2026). I am writing to request that a correction notice be added to the preprint.

The preprint contains a central quantitative error. It reports that the deep-time East Java sites are located 90–170 km from volcanic centres. That figure is incorrect: it was produced from an incomplete volcano inventory containing only seven eastern East Java centres, which omitted Lawu, Wilis and the whole of Central Java. Recomputed against the complete inventory of thirty Javanese volcanic centres, the distances are 33–53 km. The error was identified in peer review, and the reviewers were correct. Because this distance claim underpins the manuscript's argument, the interpretation built on it does not stand.

I understand from your "Notice to Authors" that self-service revision of existing preprints is currently disabled, which is why I am requesting your assistance rather than revising the preprint directly.

I would be grateful if you could add the notice below to the preprint page, so that readers — in particular anyone who has cited it — are alerted. If a visible notice is not technically possible, I would instead request that the preprint be withdrawn, with the notice text as the public statement of the reason. I would prefer the notice, as a silent withdrawal is less useful to readers who have already cited the work.

Proposed notice text:

"This preprint contains an error that invalidates its central quantitative claim, and readers should not rely on it. The manuscript states that deep-time archaeological sites in East Java are located 90–170 km from volcanic centres. That figure is incorrect: it was produced from an incomplete volcano inventory of seven eastern East Java centres, which omitted Lawu, Wilis and the whole of Central Java. Recomputed against a complete inventory of thirty Javanese volcanic centres, the distances are 33–53 km. The error was identified in peer review, and the reviewers were correct. Because the distance claim underpins the manuscript's argument, the interpretation built on it does not stand."

Thank you for your help.
