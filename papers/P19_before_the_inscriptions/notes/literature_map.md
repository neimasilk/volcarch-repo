## Tier 1: MUST READ & ENGAGE (cite and respond to in paper)

### The Indianization Debate

| # | Work | Status | Relevance to P19 |
|---|------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Wolters, O.W.** (1999) *History, Culture, and Region in Southeast Asian Perspectives* (rev. ed.). Cornell SEAP. | TO READ | "Localization" theory — the RECEIVING culture that localized Indian imports is our missing layer. Ch. 1 + Ch. 5. |
| 2 | **Coedes, George** (1968) *The Indianized States of Southeast Asia*. ANU Press. | FAMILIAR | The "Indianization" model P19 critiques. Frame as historically important but epistemologically limited. |
| 3 | **Kulke, Hermann** (1990) "The Early and the Imperial Kingdom in Southeast Asian History" in Marr & Milner eds. | TO READ | Mandala theory presupposes EXISTING political structures that adopted Indian models. |
| 4 | **van Leur, J.C.** (1955) *Indonesian Trade and Society*. van Hoeve. | TO READ | Early critique of Indianization-as-imposition. "Thin, easily flaking glaze." |

| # | Work | Status | Relevance to P19 |

|---|------|--------|-------------------|

|---|------|--------|-------------------|
