## Notice text (paste at the top of the record description)

> **Correction (1 October 2026).** Lines ~401–405 of this preprint report that, after three
> survey-intensity proxies were controlled for, "volcanic proximity retains a significant negative
> residual effect on site density" (β = −0.477, p = 0.0015, n = 703 grid cells), and conclude that "the
> volcanic site deficit is not reducible to differential survey effort". The coefficient in that model
> is on *distance* to the nearest volcano. A negative value therefore means that recorded sites become
> **more** numerous closer to volcanoes once the survey proxies are controlled, not fewer. The sentence
> states the opposite of what the analysis shows and does not support a survey-independent volcanic
> deficit. The site inventory used in that regression (an OpenStreetMap/Wikipedia compilation with
> almost no period information) also includes modern monuments. We withdraw the sentence and the
> conclusion drawn from it. Other parts of the preprint are being re-audited, and any further
> corrections will be posted as a new version. Details and the re-analysis:
> https://github.com/neimasilk/volcarch-repo — `experiments/E069_adversarial_comparanda/adv3_survey_intensity/README.md`
> (section "CORRECTION 2026-10-01").
