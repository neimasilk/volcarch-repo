\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit the manuscript ``Mini-NusaRC: A Georeferenced Archaeological Site Database for Island Southeast Asia and Madagascar (1,200--1,600,000 BP)'' for consideration as a Data Paper in the \textit{Journal of Open Archaeology Data}.

This paper presents Mini-NusaRC, a georeferenced compilation of 80 dated archaeological sites across eight regions of Island Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Timor-Leste) and Madagascar. To my knowledge, no standardized, georeferenced database of dated archaeological sites currently exists for this region --- despite its global significance for hominin evolution, modern human dispersal, and Austronesian expansion.

Mini-NusaRC covers five hominin species (\textit{H. sapiens}, \textit{H. erectus}, \textit{H. floresiensis}, \textit{H. luzonensis}, and unidentified tool-makers), seven dating methods, and a temporal span from 1,200 to 1,600,000 BP. The database includes both volcanic regions (Java, Sumatra, Flores, Philippines) and non-volcanic controls (Kalimantan, Madagascar), enabling the first systematic comparison of archaeological site distributions across volcanic and non-volcanic landscapes in Island Southeast Asia.

The dataset is designed as a seed for a comprehensive Nusantaran chronometric database and has been deposited on Zenodo [DOI to be inserted after upload] under a CC BY 4.0 license, in CSV format.
